Privacy Disclaimer

WolfDecode runs locally in your browser. It does not upload, transmit, or remotely store the input, output, or Decode Track. Nothing is recorded by WolfDecode on the developer end. Data is saved to your device only when you choose Save .txt. Avoid saving sensitive data unless you intend to keep a local copy.
